Bodycam - Permissions and Credits
by AverageBENNAL


Permissions

Fine without asking:
- Mod packs and collections (Nexus Collections, Wabbajack, etc). A link back
  to the Nexus page is appreciated.
- Videos, streams, screenshots and reviews.

Message me first:
- Reuploading it anywhere other than its Nexus page.
- Reusing the plugin, its code or its files in another mod.
- Patches, translations, ports or anything built on top of it. I'll most
  likely say yes.

Not allowed:
- Charging for it or putting it behind a paywall.
- Claiming it as your own work.

Provided as is, with no warranty. It hooks the game at a low level, so keep
backups of your saves like with any F4SE plugin. Full terms are in the LICENSE
file on GitHub.

Bodycam is a fan mod. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Reissad Studio
(makers of the game "Bodycam") or Bethesda Softworks / ZeniMax Media.


Requirements

- Fallout 4 Anniversary Edition 1.11.240 with F4SE 0.7.9, or Old-Gen 1.10.163
  with F4SE 0.6.23. Pick your version in the installer; the wrong build just
  won't load.
- Mod Configuration Menu (MCM).
- Microsoft Visual C++ 2015-2022 Redistributable (x64). If Bodycam doesn't
  load and its log is empty, install this first.


Compatibility

- See-Through-Scopes: works. 3D scopes are treated like iron sights, so free
  aim, bob and recoil apply while scoped.
- HUDFramework: works. The crosshair is found by name.
- Recoil add-ons registering through Bodycam's recoil interface replace the
  built-in recoil.
- No ESP, so it can be installed or removed at any time. Movement speed is
  never changed.
